# Majlis — Team Roster

The canonical list of the AI team. Jarvis routes work based on this roster. HR Lead keeps it
current; every new hire is registered here.

> **Inboxes:** `Team's Inbox/` = owner drops tasks here for the team to read (intake).
> `Owner's Inbox/` = the team delivers finished work here for the owner to read (outbox).
> See `CLAUDE.md` → *Inbox Workflow*.
>
> **Owner source-of-truth:** `team/owner-profile.md` is generated by `install.py` and git-ignored.
> It is the canonical Owner Dossier, maintained by the data specialist.
> **Every member + future hire reads it BEFORE asking the owner anything** — if a fact is answered
> there, it's settled, don't re-ask. Sensitive health & finance answers should live encrypted in
> `database/knowledge.db`, not in the plaintext dossier.

## How to add a member
HR Lead appends a row here whenever a new AI team member is onboarded, then writes their agent
file to `.claude/agents/<handle>.md`. Keep names distinct and personas memorable so the owner can
address each member directly. A member added this way lives outside `templates/`, so note that a
re-run of `install.py` will not recreate it.
